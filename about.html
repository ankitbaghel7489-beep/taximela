Taximela connects travellers with drivers for one way, round trip, local and airport rides. You see the fare before you pay, and you know what it covers.

Why we built Taximela

Booking a cab for a longer trip is often a guessing game. The price changes on the phone, the return fare appears out of nowhere, and toll turns into an argument at the first plaza.

Taximela is built to remove that. You enter where you are going, see every car with the fare for your exact distance, and pay a small advance to lock it in. The rest goes to the driver when you travel.

We focus on the trips where clarity matters most: one way and round trip journeys between cities, local rentals by the package, and airport transfers.

What you can expect

  • ₹
    Fares you can read

    Distance, toll and state tax treatment, and parking are shown on every car before you pay.

  • ✓
    Drivers we have reviewed

    Drivers join Taximela after our team checks their details. Your driver’s name, number and car are shared with you once the trip is accepted.

  • ☎
    Someone to talk to

    If something is unclear before or after your trip, reach us from the contact page and we will help.
