Questions, answered

How much do I pay to book?

A minimum advance, shown on every car, confirms your booking. You can pay more now and pay less to the driver.

Is toll included in the fare?

For outstation trips you choose toll and state tax included or excluded before you pay. Local rental fares exclude toll and state tax. Parking is always paid by the customer.

How do I get my driver’s details?

Once a driver accepts your trip, we share the driver’s name, phone number and car details on your mobile number. Make sure your WhatsApp number is correct when you book.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Our Refund & Cancellation policy explains how refunds work.

Do you offer local and airport rides?

Yes. Choose Local / Airport on the booking card to book a local rental package or an airport transfer.
